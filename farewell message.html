A note on uniting the right

Thank you for visiting Stop the Left. This site was built to make one simple case: that where the right-of-centre vote divides, the left wins by default — and that voters deserve a clear, honest guide to backing whichever right-of-centre party is best placed in their own constituency.

That argument has now found a larger home. A second site, Unite the Right, has launched with the same goal and far greater reach, and it makes the case better resourced than a solo project ever could.

Just as there isn't room for two right-of-centre parties to split each seat, there isn't room for two right-of-centre tactical voting sites to split the cause.

So rather than divide the very effort this project exists to unite, Stop the Left is stepping aside and pointing you to where that work now continues. The principle matters more than the banner it flies under.

If you came here to find your tactical vote, you'll find it there — covering every seat in Great Britain.
