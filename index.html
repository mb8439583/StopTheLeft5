    Why this site exists

    In the run-up to the 2024 election, the left-wing tactical voting site Stop the Tories (now Stop Reform and the Tories) surged in popularity, helping left-wing voters coalesce behind whoever was best placed to win each seat. In the interests of democratic fairness, right-of-centre voters deserve the same option — and so this site was created.
